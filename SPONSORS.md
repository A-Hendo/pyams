# GitHub Sponsor Tiers

Thank you for considering supporting PYAMS! Your contributions help keep the project free, open-source, and actively maintained.

## Monthly Tiers

### 💖 Backer ($2/mo)
Show your support for the project. Every bit helps keep development moving forward.

### 🌟 Supporter ($5/mo)
A great way to say thanks! Helps cover basic maintenance and infrastructure costs.

### 🚀 Sponsor ($10/mo)
Supports active development of new features and improved cross-platform compatibility.

### 🏛️ Sustainer ($25/mo)
A significant contribution that helps ensure the long-term sustainability of the project.

## One-time Tiers

### ☕ Buy me a coffee ($5)
A simple "thank you" for making your media server setup a little easier.

### 🍕 Pizza Night ($15)
A generous tip that fuels late-night bug hunting and feature building.
